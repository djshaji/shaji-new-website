## About me

I teach in the Department of English at Government SPMR College of Commerce, Jammu. My undergraduate courses include British literature, Shakespeare, Indian Writing in English, postcolonial literature, and journalism. I hold an M.Phil. and an M.A. in English and qualified UGC NET in English.

My research looks at what computational methods can reveal about literary form. I also founded the Machine Learning for Literature Consortium and the SPMR Programmer’s Club.
