*Savera* is an acoustic single. Visit my [music website](https://music.shaji.in) or [YouTube channel](https://www.youtube.com/@ShajiKhanMusic) for music, performances and sessions.

## How I record

I track, mix and master in **Ardour** on Linux and program drum arrangements in **Hydrogen**. For guitar tone, I use custom LADSPA and LV2 plugin chains and [Amp Rack](code.md), the open-source effects processor I develop.
