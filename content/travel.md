## Jammu to Goa and Kanyakumari

*December 2025 – January 2026*

I drove from Jammu to Panaji and on to Kanyakumari, the southern tip of mainland India. The trip included stretches of **NH 44** and stops in Jaipur, Daman and Alibaug.

## Preparing the touring car

I drive an Oxford Blue Maruti Suzuki Dzire. For long highway days, I added interior sound insulation and a firewall silencer to reduce cabin noise and vibration.

## What I look for along the way

Architecture and regional culture shape where I stop. I document places as a Google Maps Local Guide and often use academic travel as an opportunity to explore by road.
