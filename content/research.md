## Selected projects

### Structural & Rhythmic Elasticity in Blank Verse

*Completed manuscript · November 2026*

This study examines rhythmic variation and affective shifts in Shakespeare and Marlowe using transformer embeddings, principal component analysis, metrical entropy and terminal surprisal.

### From Blank Verse to Polyphony

*Completed manuscript · November 2026*

A computational framework for translating Shakespearean soliloquies and dramatic dialogue into contrapuntal structures in MusicXML and MIDI.

### Cross-Lingual Topologies of Dissent

*Research in progress*

A comparative study of dissent and anti-colonial poetry using cross-lingual computational and stylometric methods.

### Computational Literary Studies textbook

*Book proposal and drafting*

A practical introduction to computational text analysis for literature students, with exercises in Python, pandas, spaCy and transformer-based analysis.
